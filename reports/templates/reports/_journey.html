Rules used

Sessions
A session is one browser's events less than 30 minutes apart; 30 minutes without activity starts a new one. A new campaign, a new referrer or midnight does not. Each session is one touch, credited to its first page view's UTMs, or to its referrer when it has none (as in Dreamdata and GA4). Its other page views are listed under it and never counted again.
Identity
An identify links earlier anonymous views of the same anonymousId to its contact. A browser identified as two contacts links to neither.
Time
Event time is corrected for the browser's clock error: receive time minus sentAt.

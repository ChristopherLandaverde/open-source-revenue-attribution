Rules used

Visits
A page view starts a visit when it is the contact's first, comes over 30 minutes after the previous one, carries a new campaign, or arrives from another site. Visit starts and views with campaign data are touches; the rest is internal navigation.
Identity
An identify links earlier anonymous views of the same anonymousId to its contact. A browser identified as two contacts links to neither.
Time
Event time is corrected for the browser's clock error: receive time minus sentAt.
